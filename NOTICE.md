## USCIS Study Materials

The civics questions and answers are based on publicly available USCIS study materials for the naturalization civics test.

Users should verify official test rules, question lists, and current answers directly with USCIS.

## Current Officials

Some civics answers depend on current elected or appointed officials.

These answers may change over time, including:

- President
- Vice President
- Speaker of the House
- Chief Justice
- Governor
- U.S. Senators
- U.S. Representative

Users should verify these answers with official government sources before relying on them for test preparation.

## No Legal Advice

This project does not provide immigration or legal advice.

For official immigration questions, users should consult USCIS or a qualified immigration professional.

## Privacy

This app stores progress locally in the user's browser using Local Storage.

The repository does not include a backend server or user account system.
